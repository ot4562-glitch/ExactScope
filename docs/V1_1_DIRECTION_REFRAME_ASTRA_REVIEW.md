# ExactScope v1.1 Direction Reframe — Astra High Review

## 1. Executive verdict

**Continue a bounded evidence program; do not resume broad product implementation or promote a new technical category yet.** The best direction is Thesis B as an internal design hypothesis, Thesis C as the intended commercial offer, and Thesis A as the first conservative selection algorithm. This is a conditional research recommendation, not a finding that ExactScope has established an independent category.

The proposed boundary is coherent: decide which bounded evidence and answer contract reach an existing model, enforce the resulting contract, and bind the policy to prospective qualification evidence. But existing program optimizers and RAG frameworks can express essentially all these decisions. Moving them into a separate artifact creates a useful engineering boundary; it does not establish exclusive technical territory or a business.

**Today, ExactScope is best understood as a constrained configuration optimizer with semantic enforcement and qualification records.** It would earn a stronger compiler/product interpretation through reusable semantics, lower integration effort, and prospective selection advantages across changing workloads and hosts. These remain unproved.

The first fresh experiment demonstrated rejection of an unsuccessful candidate. It did not demonstrate successful distillation, a validated revised selector, or customer savings. The reframe improves the specification of what to test, rather than the amount of positive evidence.

Proceed with a modified 120/600 FEVER study as a bounded falsification of within-workload selection. Add a conventional tuning comparator, clarify competence and statistical scope, and retain the existing product thresholds. A pass permits a customer-like retrieval study. It does not permit release qualification or a cross-runtime claim. A valid failure should stop further FEVER selector repair on the same evidence and force a business decision before additional implementation.

**Scope and evidence discipline.** This review read the direction-reframe packet completely. Targeted checks used the product-differentiation document, research status, prior Astra review, and experiment program to verify selector semantics, accepted gates, oracle retrieval, runtime evidence, and intervention claims. No implementation or raw experiment artifacts were inspected. “Demonstrated” below means reported experimental evidence in those documents, not an independent rerun. Current official external documentation was consulted for the collision analysis; it is not an exhaustive competitive survey. Incumbent replication difficulty and economic judgments are architectural inferences. Proposed schemas, thresholds for future stages, and investment cutoffs are recommendations. Moat and buyer-demand claims are hypotheses or speculation. Scores are subjective evidence assessments, not probabilities.

## 2. What the previous low scores actually meant

The scores primarily reflected **insufficient evidence, an unproved selection advantage, and a weak commercial wedge**. They did not establish that host-attached semantic processing is a fundamentally bad architecture.

- **Architecture:** leaving model execution and retrieval infrastructure with the host is sensible. The unresolved architecture problem was the exact independent object ExactScope owns and the obligations that object can actually enforce.
- **Category definition:** “amplifier,” “distillation,” and “compiler” bundled prompt simplification, evidence processing, runtime hints, and evaluation into a larger claim than their common abstraction justified. The new language narrows this ambiguity but does not eliminate overlap.
- **Evidence:** the 6+6 experiment was too small to support selection among ten policies. Its negative outcome is real; its population implications are very uncertain.
- **Commercial wedge:** no evidence establishes willingness to pay, integration effort, repeat traffic sufficient for payback, or savings after fallback and requalification.
- **Transfer:** there is no fresh successful transfer of the revised selector, and no demonstrated reduction in search effort on a new host or workload.

The reported results remain: prompt_reduced and integrated each scored 3/6 on calibration; the cheaper prompt_reduced was selected; held-out Base and prompt_reduced each scored 2/6 while integrated scored 3/6; qualification rejected the candidate. The preservation rule was designed after this failure. Neither the old held-out nor the subsequently selected A+D configuration validates that rule.

The packet is right to preserve useful borrowed mechanisms. However, a collection of useful mechanisms can belong in someone else's framework. Their utility does not establish that ExactScope should own their orchestration commercially.

One packet inference needs weakening: the observed failure does not prove that aggregate selection caused the held-out loss or that paired preservation repairs the cause. It motivates a conservative alternative that still needs an independent test.

## 3. Thesis A review — Reference-Preserving Cost Compiler

**Scores:** technical coherence **65/100**; category distinctness **15/100**; compiler credibility **35/100**; product usefulness **40/100**; defensibility **15/100**; commercial clarity **50/100**.

**Verdict: MODIFY — retain as an algorithm inside the product, not the full product identity.**

The objective is precise. For calibration observations, require zero candidate losses on observed reference successes, require strictly lower cost, select deterministically, freeze the candidate, and test it independently. This is a defensible constrained empirical optimization problem.

Its strongest property is disciplined separation of candidate selection from deployment qualification. Its largest weakness is that preservation is relative to the observed success set of one reference. That is neither a population guarantee nor a proof that the reference is competent. `S[F] > 0` and `S[F] >= S[B]` merely prevent two obvious pathological cases. A reference scoring 2/120 against a Base scoring 1/120 would satisfy them and still be commercially useless.

As calibration grows, a genuinely useful candidate with occasional reference-relative losses becomes less likely to survive exact preservation. This is a deliberate conservatism/coverage tradeoff, not necessarily improved statistical efficiency. Selection can collapse into ReferenceOnly even when a customer would accept a small noninferiority margin. Do not quietly change the objective to recover a favorable result.

The algorithm also prefers whichever reference successes happen to be observed. It does not distinguish a critical error from a minor one unless that distinction is encoded in the contract and scorer before evaluation. Preserving average task success can still conceal unsupported citations or unacceptable subgroup losses.

The implementation can be short, auditable, and valuable. It is also easy for a customer or incumbent to reproduce. A generic catalog enumerator with the same constraints and tie-breaks must return the same answer. Calling that enumerator a compiler creates no additional differentiation.

An appropriate claim after a pass is: a cheaper fixed policy selected by this rule met the predeclared held-out requirements in this workload. The stronger claim that reference preservation is the best selector requires prospective comparisons, not one successful application.

## 4. Thesis B review — Semantic Inference Policy Compiler

**Scores:** technical coherence **60/100**; category distinctness **30/100**; compiler credibility **40/100**; product usefulness **45/100**; defensibility **25/100**; commercial clarity **30/100**.

**Verdict: MODIFY — accept a narrow internal abstraction; reject an established-category claim.**

There is a meaningful software layer between an application's evidence semantics and a runtime's execution interface. Its useful object is a bounded decision program with explicit preconditions, output obligations, and qualification scope. That layer is more concrete than an “amplifier” and could keep host-specific mechanics outside the semantic core.

But the packet draws too sharp a distinction from LM programs and RAG pipelines. Those programs can include evidence projection, conditional generation, validators, and typed outputs. A program optimizer need not be restricted to rewriting prompt strings. RAG frameworks can express branching and admission logic. These are overlapping representations, not disjoint categories.

**If v1.1 enumerates existing prompt/evidence settings, saves the chosen settings, and attaches a test report, it is configuration optimization under a new name.** That can still be useful. To justify “compiler,” the system must also define stable source semantics, validate transformations against those semantics, lower to host obligations, and reject unsupported deployments predictably. To justify a distinct product, it must make this process cheaper or more reliable than a competent host team's equivalent implementation.

The broad policy list should be narrowed to two related decisions:

1. **Evidence sufficiency and context admission:** select a bounded, provenance-preserving evidence delivery under explicit coverage rules, then obtain exact fit confirmation from the host. No byte-based token guesses and no arbitrary truncation.
2. **Answer-contract lowering:** express the permitted answer domain and evidence requirements, use a qualified host constraint mechanism when available, and apply the same deterministic finalization contract afterward.

These decisions should be tested across hosts and related tasks. A transferable representation is only the first step. The stronger test is whether prior knowledge predicts useful budgets or contract/capability combinations before target scoring, reducing search while retaining final quality.

Proof-based zero-call completion belongs only where the application can supply a sound derivation rule. An authoritative paragraph mentioning an answer is not itself proof that the answer resolves a natural-language question. Profile qualification does not prove a new request. For ordinary open-ended QA, leave completion disabled unless a specific rule is justified.

Qualification of caches or constrained decoding must also separate the host's documented semantic guarantees, local conformance checks, sampled quality evidence, and measured economics. Sampled output parity is not proof of universal equivalence. Do not require ExactScope to rediscover every guarantee already owned by the runtime.

## 5. Thesis C review — Qualified Execution Profile Compiler

**Scores:** technical coherence **65/100**; category distinctness **25/100**; compiler credibility **35/100**; product usefulness **50/100**; defensibility **20/100**; commercial clarity **60/100**.

**Verdict: MODIFY — strongest customer framing, provided qualification and savings are explicitly scoped.**

The buyer can understand a service that tests cheaper ways to run an existing application and produces an auditable deployable profile. The word “semantic” contributes little to this buying decision. “Qualified execution profile” names a deliverable and allows a customer to retain control of its runtime and failure handling.

However, “preserve required quality” must mean specified metrics and tolerances on a specified population under specified conditions. It must not imply every answer is correct or every reference success is preserved in production. A failed compilation can yield no usable profile. A commercial offer must explain that outcome before the customer incurs substantial setup cost.

The attractive promises are operational constraints, not unique capabilities. An optimizer can use an expensive reflection model offline and still deploy a one-call program. Therefore no second model or judge **at serving time** is not a categorical advantage over DSPy/GEPA. ExactScope's intended absence of mandatory offline models may simplify costs, but the customer cares about total payback and the resulting deployed behavior.

“Low integration and operational ownership” is currently unsupported. Evidence schemas, authority rules, tokenizer callbacks, qualification refresh, and fallback accounting may require substantial host work. Shipping a tiny file does not make that work disappear.

Start with a bounded paid evaluation/profile-delivery offer if a suitable customer exists. Do not build a broad hosted control plane before demonstrating repeated value. A project-based offer can establish demand, but repeated bespoke delivery without reusable selection or integration would be a services business, not evidence of a compiler moat.

## 6. Adjacent-system collision analysis

The capability descriptions below are grounded in linked primary sources. Replication difficulty, economic relevance, and proposed differentiators are this review's judgments. No claim of exclusive white space follows from the absence of a feature on a documentation page.

**DSPy / GEPA / LM program optimization.** DSPy optimizes LM program behavior against a metric; GEPA uses execution feedback to propose and evaluate instructions during compilation. Its reflection work occurs offline. [DSPy GEPA tutorial](https://dspy.ai/getting-started/gepa-optimization/), [GEPA API](https://dspy.ai/api/optimizers/GEPA/overview/).

- **Their object / ExactScope's object:** an optimized LM program versus a restricted semantic policy plus deployment qualification obligations.
- **Overlap:** metric-driven search, structured LM interfaces, saved programs, and calibration-derived behavior. Prompt-only optimization is already occupied.
- **Technical significance:** restricted semantics and a small host contract may be useful, but ExactScope policies can be represented inside a larger LM program. This is a specialization, not an expressiveness advantage.
- **Economic significance:** only if the specialization saves integration, labeling, search, or recurring qualification effort for a real workload.
- **Incumbent addition:** a frozen profile exporter and qualification gate are relatively easy additions; robust cross-host evidence and maintained workload contracts require more sustained effort.
- **Evidence beyond a wrapper:** matched-budget comparison against a competent optimizer under the same final gates, or equivalent deployed quality/cost with demonstrably less integration and search. A comparator must be allowed offline optimization while respecting the one-call serving constraint.

**RAG pipeline frameworks.** Haystack already provides connected components, branches, loops, connection validation, and serialized pipelines. [Haystack pipelines](https://docs.haystack.deepset.ai/docs/pipelines).

- **Their object / ExactScope's object:** the retrieval/generation workflow versus a bounded contract at the evidence-to-model boundary.
- **Overlap:** evidence selection, prompt construction, routing, validation, and persisted behavior. “Decide whether to call the model” is false white space.
- **Technical significance:** a smaller integration surface may permit attachment to an existing pipeline without adopting another orchestration framework. ExactScope still needs evidence fetching to obey the selected policy.
- **Economic significance:** plausible for teams unwilling to replace a mature stack, provided the attachment takes little work and produces net savings.
- **Incumbent addition:** a policy component or pipeline template is easy; maintaining qualified behavior across unrelated stacks is harder but not inherently exclusive.
- **Evidence beyond a wrapper:** an independently integrated profile improves economics in an existing customer pipeline and requires less host-specific code than the equivalent framework component. Do not claim value simply from serializing a subset of a pipeline.

**RAG evaluation / grounding systems.** Haystack exposes statistical and model-based document/answer evaluators, including exact-match, retrieval, and faithfulness measures. [Haystack evaluation](https://docs.haystack.deepset.ai/docs/evaluation).

- **Their object / ExactScope's object:** measurements of retrieval and answer quality versus a fixed decision policy accompanied by an auditable acceptance decision.
- **Overlap:** grounding scores, evidence relevance, regression detection, and gate construction. Separate retrieval/generation evaluation is already occupied.
- **Technical significance:** deterministic request-time checks differ from offline quality measurement, but structural validity or citation membership does not prove entailment.
- **Economic significance:** useful if enforcing a narrow application contract prevents costly invalid outputs without excessive rejection or extra model calls.
- **Incumbent addition:** deterministic validators and release gates are straightforward. Domain-specific sound proof rules are harder, but their value may belong to the application owner.
- **Evidence beyond a wrapper:** measured reduction in real contract failures while preserving completed-task coverage, with explicit limits on what the validator proves. Reuse existing evaluators rather than building another general evaluation library.

**Generic prompt/program configuration tuning.** General optimization tooling already supports multi-objective search and Pareto selection. [Optuna multi-objective tutorial](https://optuna.readthedocs.io/en/stable/tutorial/20_recipes/002_multi_objective.html).

- **Their object / ExactScope's object:** a parameterized executable and objectives/constraints versus a constrained catalog of semantic transformations with qualification identity.
- **Overlap:** nearly the entire current selector. Paired preservation can be implemented as an ordinary feasibility predicate.
- **Technical significance:** negligible if the only difference is parameter names. Meaningful if stable semantic types and legal lowering rules reduce invalid choices and integration errors.
- **Economic significance:** comes from reusable interventions, reduced search, and deployment maintenance, not the minimization algorithm.
- **Incumbent addition:** easy to copy the algorithm and profile format. This is the strongest direct collision.
- **Evidence beyond a wrapper:** priors or semantic constraints find equally good qualified policies with fewer observations than a cold tuner on untouched target combinations. If identical constraints produce identical output, honestly describe the optimizer as replaceable infrastructure.

**vLLM / TensorRT-LLM / SGLang.** These projects own substantial model-serving execution and performance surfaces; TensorRT-LLM explicitly exposes KV-cache management. They must remain owners of execution mechanics. [vLLM documentation](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/stable/), [TensorRT-LLM KV cache](https://nvidia.github.io/TensorRT-LLM/features/kvcache.html), [SGLang documentation](https://docs.sglang.io/).

- **Their object / ExactScope's object:** efficient execution of model work versus application-specific evidence and answer obligations governing that work.
- **Overlap:** structured output eligibility, prefix behavior, serving-cost measurement, and performance configuration. The boundary is porous; runtime ecosystems can incorporate application-aware tooling.
- **Technical significance:** real for source authority and application answer semantics, which a generic scheduler cannot infer. Weak for merely toggling cache or speculation settings.
- **Economic significance:** reducing necessary model work can complement faster execution, but token reductions may not release paid capacity and may change batching or cache benefits.
- **Incumbent addition:** application-specific semantics require customer input; capability presets and benchmark-based tuning are easy extensions. Runtime vendors possess stronger engine-specific performance knowledge.
- **Evidence beyond a wrapper:** incremental gains over an already well-tuned runtime under matched load, including cache effects and total host work. Abandon ownership of cache, scheduling, kernels, decoding, and accelerator selection.

**ONNX Runtime / ExecuTorch / LiteRT / OpenVINO.** ORT GenAI includes generation-loop controls, sampling, chat-template support, and structured-output support. ExecuTorch and LiteRT-LM provide on-device LLM execution paths; OpenVINO provides generative-AI deployment tooling. Capabilities are product/version-specific, not interchangeable. [ORT GenAI](https://onnxruntime.ai/docs/genai/), [ExecuTorch Android LLM guide](https://docs.pytorch.org/executorch/stable/llm/run-on-android.html), [LiteRT-LM](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/LiteRT-LM), [OpenVINO generative workflow](https://docs.openvino.ai/2025/openvino-workflow-generative.html).

- **Their object / ExactScope's object:** portable model execution and platform integration versus portable policy semantics with separately qualified bindings.
- **Overlap:** host interfaces, generation options, context admission, and portable delivery. Adapter availability is false white space.
- **Technical significance:** one policy representation can separate application obligations from runtime APIs. Matching capability flags does not establish interchangeable generation quality.
- **Economic significance:** plausible for an application that actually changes runtimes or ships several targets. Irrelevant to many single-stack buyers.
- **Incumbent addition:** local semantic helpers are easy; cross-vendor qualification requires maintenance and evidence. Customers may reasonably prefer their own small binding.
- **Evidence beyond a wrapper:** real models on different runtimes, comparable semantics, measured integration work, and prior-informed requalification that beats cold search. The reported ORT execution, ExecuTorch fake-runner/header checks, and LiteRT fixture failures do not establish that result.

**Generic benchmark/evaluation harnesses.** The EleutherAI harness already provides configurable tasks, model integrations, execution, and scoring. [LM Evaluation Harness](https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness).

- **Their object / ExactScope's object:** reproducible measurements of model/task configurations versus a production-consumable policy with validity and failure semantics.
- **Overlap:** dataset identities, scorer versions, reproducibility, result aggregation, and regression testing.
- **Technical significance:** a deployed contract differs from a benchmark report, but saving the best configuration and enforcing a threshold is a modest extension.
- **Economic significance:** only if ExactScope closes the gap between evaluation and low-effort deployment in a specific application.
- **Incumbent addition:** easy to add exporters and gates; maintaining meaningful customer contracts is the harder part.
- **Evidence beyond a wrapper:** independently maintained host integrations consuming the artifact successfully, with measured qualification savings. Reuse harness execution infrastructure where possible.

**Observability/evaluation products.** LangSmith already covers offline predeployment testing, regression comparison, production monitoring, and evaluation feedback cycles. [LangSmith evaluation concepts](https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/evaluation-concepts).

- **Their object / ExactScope's object:** traces, datasets, evaluations, and application lifecycle visibility versus an executable policy and its qualification decision.
- **Overlap:** quality/cost monitoring, experiment comparison, audit trails, and deployment gates.
- **Technical significance:** the executable semantic contract is a narrower deliverable, not a replacement observability platform.
- **Economic significance:** uncertain because these vendors already own the evaluation workflow and budget relationship. ExactScope must justify an additional purchase.
- **Incumbent addition:** recommending and exporting a qualified configuration is a credible extension. Distribution and access to customer traces favor incumbents.
- **Evidence beyond a wrapper:** repeated savings or search reductions unavailable from existing evaluation workflows, delivered through integration with those products. Do not build another tracing UI or dataset-management platform.

**True potential white space:** a maintained, narrowly typed evidence-to-model policy contract plus prospective knowledge that reduces the cost of qualifying it across changing hosts. **False white space:** immutable configuration files, offline optimization, one-call deployment, routing, output validation, evaluation gates, or runtime adapters in isolation. Explicitly abandon claims to own these capabilities as a novel category.

## 7. Exact artifact ExactScope should compile

Compile **an immutable candidate policy and, only after a pass, a separate immutable qualification attestation referencing its digest**. The deployed qualified profile is the pair. Do not mutate a candidate's `qualified` flag after testing or allow a report to qualify a different artifact. Keep large evidence records outside the hot-path payload.

The following field groups are a proposed contract, not an assertion about current implementation:

```text
policy:
  schema_version, policy_id, canonicalization_version, content_digest
  semantic_policy:
    workload_contract_id, contract_version
    query_rule: registered_rule_id + frozen_parameters
    retrieval_request: candidate_limit, required_candidate_fields
    evidence_rule: transform_id, version, ordering, bounded_parameters
    coverage_rule_id, authority_rule_id, freshness_rule_id, conflict_rule_id
    allowed_answer_domain, citation_requirements, abstention_semantics
    semantic_instruction_id_or_digest
    completion_rule_id_or_disabled
  host_requirements:
    evidence_interface_version, policy_executor_version
    exact_rendered_fit_callback_version, output_token_reserve
    required_capabilities, qualified_optional_capability_bindings
    structured_output_dialect_and_supported_subset
    bounded_generation_settings, max_model_calls
  deterministic_rules:
    ordered_admission_steps, ordered_evidence_tiers
    completion_verifier_id_or_disabled
    finalizer_id_and_version, rejection_reason_enum
    on_missing_capability, on_no_fit, on_identity_mismatch

qualification_attestation:
  schema_version, attestation_id, policy_digest
  qualification_identity:
    model_artifact_and_quantization_ids
    runtime_build, tokenizer_digest, chat_template_digest
    adapter_and_executor_digests, generation_settings_digest
    retrieval_snapshot_and_config_id, evidence_schema_id
    workload_population_and_grouping_id, capability_record_digest
    hardware_and_load_regime_id_for_cost_and_latency
  evidence:
    preregistration_digest, calibration_manifest_digest
    heldout_manifest_digest, scorer_and_analysis_digests
    base_id, reference_id, selector_id, catalog_digest
    paired_counts, bounds, coverage_and_rejection_counts
    contract_violation_counts, qualification_decision
    restricted_access_report_reference
  economics:
    cost_model_id, accounting_units, coefficient_digest
    serving_costs_and_ratios, latency_protocol_and_results
    compilation_and_qualification_cost, measured_integration_hours
  validity:
    explicit_scope, excluded_conditions, invalidation_rules
    qualification_time, review_or_expiry_policy
```

Every registered rule needs bounded inputs, deterministic behavior, and a versioned implementation digest. Do not accept arbitrary executable plugins in a profile as a shortcut to a universal language. The artifact can identify a host implementation without embedding it. Store only optional capability combinations actually qualified; absence must not silently select an untested alternative. The minimal initial profile should leave cache, speculation, and zero-call completion disabled unless the chosen experiment needs them.

**Request-time data belongs in a separate receipt.** Bind request ID, policy digest, host identity, authorized evidence snapshot, selected source/version/span IDs, semantic delivery digest, answer contract, fit confirmation, and any proof to the current operation. Carry this receipt into finalization. This prevents accepting an output against the wrong delivery or a stale proof. Keep it request-scoped rather than accumulating customer content in a reusable profile.

**Resolve retrieval timing explicitly.** Overfetch cannot be decided after `prepare` has received too few candidates. The host must read the profile's bounded retrieval request before retrieving, or support one specified pre-generation fetch exchange. Start with the former. The host performs retrieval and access filtering; ExactScope validates the resulting records and shapes evidence. A multi-stage adaptive retriever is outside the minimum product.

**Meaning of the outcomes:** `Complete(answer, proof, receipt)` is permitted only after a registered verifier succeeds on current authorized evidence. `Generate(delivery, contract, requirements, receipt)` obliges the host to render and confirm exact fit before its one model call. `Accept(answer, receipt)` means the declared checks passed; any unproved semantic truth remains unproved. `Reject` and `Unavailable` carry stable reasons and never initiate fallback themselves.

**Do not store or own:** model weights, tokenizer or template implementations, inference kernels, KV state, cache eviction policy, schedulers, batching, speculative decoders, vector databases, access-control policy, customer credentials, raw calibration answers in the hot artifact, or unrestricted customer corpora. Identity digests of these dependencies are legitimate. The host owns authorization, exact rendering/tokenization, model invocation, runtime enforcement, fallback, and operational activation/rollback. ExactScope cannot prevent a host from violating its call contract; qualification assumes and checks a conforming host.

## 8. Compiler thesis

“Compiler” is credible as an engineering description when there is a specified source-to-artifact transformation with defined semantics. It is not synonymous with novel optimization, and it does not require generating native code.

- **Input language / representation:** a restricted workload contract, typed evidence records, host capability/identity declarations, a versioned intervention catalog, and an objective with qualification constraints. “Workload semantics” must be supplied in this representation; ExactScope has no demonstrated automatic method for extracting trustworthy semantics from arbitrary prose or traffic.
- **Transformation / selection:** type-check obligations; reject unsupported capabilities; remove definitionally duplicate policies; bind registered semantic rules to host interfaces; execute the bounded calibration catalog; apply the declared selector; partially evaluate fixed choices into a small decision program. Record lineage. Search is one compiler pass, and may use an external optimizer.
- **Compiled artifact:** the immutable candidate and eventual attestation from section 7. A selected prompt string alone is an optimized prompt, not evidence of the broader compiler thesis.
- **Validity / qualification scope:** distinguish static conformance, per-request verified properties, and empirical population quality. Only the first two can justify their specified deterministic invariants. Held-out performance qualifies a distribution and configuration within uncertainty; it does not prove universal semantic equivalence.
- **Deterministic runtime semantics:** identical normalized request, evidence, profile, and host callback answers produce the same preparation and finalization decisions. Model outputs and scheduler timing need not be deterministic. Branching on a frozen rule is compatible with a fixed policy; adaptive learning or candidate reselection at serving time is not part of v1.1.
- **Failure semantics:** calibration returns NoQualifiedReference, ReferenceOnly, or CompiledCandidate. Qualification returns qualified or rejected for that exact artifact. Runtime incompatibility returns Unavailable/Reject. No silent truncation, repair generation, alternate policy, or promotion of the reference after candidate failure. The host may follow its own fallback policy, with its costs and outcomes measured separately.
- **Recompilation triggers:** changes to semantic rules, workload contract, intervention definitions, or selected choices require compilation and qualification of a new candidate. Model, tokenizer/template, runtime, retriever, or capability changes invalidate the affected attestation and require requalification; the semantic policy may remain unchanged. Price/load changes can invalidate economics without invalidating deterministic contract semantics. Distribution changes require scope review and fresh evidence even when all binary hashes match.

The strongest compiler demonstration would show that one source contract lowers correctly to two different host mechanisms, invalid combinations are rejected before generation, and prior policy knowledge reduces fresh target qualification work. Merely running independently tuned configurations on two runtimes is compatibility evidence.

The ownership boundary is therefore correct, with one qualification: native-free-first means **no new mandatory native semantic library without a measured need**, not that every host binding must avoid its platform's native API. A thin binding may be necessary. Building another runtime is not.

## 9. Moat analysis

**Current judgment: plausible learning asset, no demonstrated moat.** If every new workload/host needs a full cold search, the proposed knowledge base is an experiment archive. It may improve developer productivity, but it has not established the claimed compounding selection advantage.

**Strongest version.** Before seeing target outcomes, normalized workload descriptors and host capabilities predict which intervention combinations are likely to preserve quality economically. Predictions include uncertainty and negative interactions. The system proposes fewer candidates, asks for fewer calibration labels where justified, and avoids known integration mistakes while maintaining the same independent qualification standard. Each genuinely new task improves this prediction process. The proprietary value would be prospective, reproducible interaction outcomes and their useful normalization, not a large count of stored runs.

**Weakest version.** Every record is tied to benchmark IDs, a prompt, a model revision, and one engineer's choices. Positive outcomes are published while failed searches disappear. “Transfer” means copying a hand-written recipe and then exhaustively retuning it. Runtime version changes invalidate most records, customers cannot share usable evidence, and incumbents learn equivalent presets from their own much larger installed bases. This is a costly maintenance catalog with little defensibility.

The cross-product is large: workload × authority regime × retriever × model × template × hardware × load × intervention interaction. A capability vector can hide crucial differences. Two hosts advertising JSON Schema support may accept different subsets or exhibit different answer quality. A cache flag says little about the traffic's prefix reuse. Start with a small number of interpretable descriptors and reject unsupported extrapolation rather than pretending to learn this entire space.

**Prospective discriminator:** freeze priors using source workloads only; register target descriptors before target scores exist; compare prior-ranked search against budget-matched cold search, a strongest reusable static recipe, and descriptor-blind pooled priors. Hold out entire workload/host combinations, including some entire domains and runtime families. Use equal final qualification data and gates for all arms. Charge all failed search and labeling effort, and log targets where the informed method finds no eligible policy. Measure candidate executions and labels needed to obtain a policy that subsequently qualifies, qualification yield, integration hours, and all-in cost.

Do not promise fewer held-out examples merely because ranking improves. Faster search can reduce time to a qualified result while the independent final test remains the same size. Reducing final qualification sample size requires a separately justified statistical protocol; prior confidence is not permission to weaken the gate.

**Breadth before public moat language:** as a proposed minimum credibility threshold, use at least **six independently sourced workloads across three domains, three genuinely different runtime families, and two model families**, with at least **four untouched target combinations spanning two held-out workloads and two runtime families**. The combinations need not form a full Cartesian product. Require prospective replication across more than one customer/integrator and at least one later runtime or model update. These counts are judgment calls, not statistical guarantees; six cosmetic benchmark variants do not qualify.

Even this supports “emerging transfer advantage” before “moat.” Defensibility additionally requires lawful reusable evidence, maintained prediction quality, sufficient customer value, and some reason competitors cannot cheaply reproduce the advantage. An observed 30% search reduction is useful evidence; it is not automatically a durable barrier.

## 10. 120/600 experiment review

**Decision: PROCEED WITH MODIFICATIONS.** Keep the same Qwen/FEVER environment and the 120/600 structure to isolate the revised selector. Do not add new capability families to make Thesis B look richer. The experiment remains an oracle-page-pooled, within-workload compiler test.

The following changes must be specified before any new scoring. This review does not change an executed preregistration. If fresh scores have already been seen, these modifications require a new untouched cohort; they cannot be retrofitted.

1. **Separate the three questions and their verdicts.** First, can A select a cheaper candidate that independently preserves F within the declared tolerance? Second, does that candidate meet the existing Base-relative product gates? Third, does ExactScope add anything over a conventional tuner? A candidate can support the narrow cost-reduction proposition while failing the stricter product proposition. Such a result emits no deployable product profile under the current gates. Report all three verdicts without relabeling failure as overall success.

2. **Define competence before calibration.** Use existing development evidence and a task-specific absolute utility floor to admit B and predeclare F. Freeze generation settings, evidence access, output contract, and scorer; explain why a skilled team would deploy each baseline. F cannot simply mean “all expensive switches enabled.” The current `S[F] > 0` and `S[F] >= S[B]` checks remain necessary empirical admission checks but are insufficient competence criteria. If no credible absolute floor can be defended, call this an algorithm diagnostic and limit the product inference accordingly. Do not invent a favorable floor after seeing scores.

3. **Add one conventional tuning comparator T using the same calibration matrix.** Predeclare T as the lowest-cost strictly cheaper feasible policy whose aggregate calibration success is at least F's, breaking ties by higher success then canonical ID; otherwise T returns F. This represents ordinary quality-constrained catalog tuning and exposes what paired preservation changes. T has the same capabilities, catalog, costs, one-call constraints, and independent final gates. Freeze T's output before held-out. T is a comparator, never a runner-up available for deployment if P fails. Also document that a generic enumerator implementing A exactly must reproduce A; that identity check exposes the lack of algorithmic novelty without another model run.

4. **Use only B, F, frozen P, and frozen T on held-out.** With ten calibration policies, the maximum is 1,200 calibration plus 2,400 held-out policy-item observations, or 3,600 generation calls at the one-call ceiling. Deduplicate identical frozen arms. If A produces ReferenceOnly or NoQualifiedReference, stop the main compiler-value study at calibration. Do not consume held-out to rescue the claim. A separate reference investigation would need its own declared purpose.

5. **Keep freshness and independence precise.** Exclude every known selector/intervention development example, including the old 12 items, and freeze near-duplicate/shared-evidence grouping. Do not treat different claim IDs as sufficient separation. Use disjoint groups for calibration and held-out. Record model-training contamination as unknown: fresh to ExactScope does not mean unseen during model pretraining. Freeze the oracle-pool construction, including treatment of insufficient-evidence claims, and prevent gold labels/evidence annotations from entering policy decisions. Oracle page access must remain an explicit limitation.

6. **Align sampling with inference.** The 120 balanced calibration claims may use 40 per label, but freeze their selection weights and do not describe that balance as the held-out population. Draw 600 held-out representatives by simple random sampling without replacement from a frozen post-exclusion frame of size N, using a label-independent within-group representative rule. The resulting estimand is that representative frame. If whole clusters or unequal weights are used instead, the current hypergeometric formulas are not automatically applicable; preregister the appropriate design-based analysis before scoring.

7. **Retain the accepted primary gates.** For P versus B, let g count P-only successes and b count B-only successes. Let r count F-success/P-failure. Apply the specified exact finite-population bounds at alpha = 0.05/3: `Lower(g) - Upper(b) >= 0.03`, `Upper(r) <= 0.02`, total serving cost at most 0.85 times F and no greater than B, E2E p95 at most 1.05 times B, and zero mandatory contract/identity/model-call violations. The reference regression denominator is all held-out requests, not only F successes; also report the latter conditional rate. Report all paired outcome cells, absolute success, evidence correctness, rejection, and coverage. Insufficient bounds fail qualification.

8. **Do prospective precision/power analysis before committing the scoring budget.** Use plausible paired-disagreement scenarios rather than the six-item result as a reliable effect estimate. Six hundred is not a universal power guarantee, particularly with separate conservative bounds for gains and losses. Verify exact interval endpoint behavior and calculate minimum attainable bounds for the actual N. If 600 cannot distinguish the minimum useful effects under defensible assumptions, replace the sample size and freeze it before scoring; never extend it afterward. Primary P qualification retains its existing error allocation. T comparisons are descriptive unless a separate predeclared inferential plan supports superiority claims; do not opportunistically claim A beats T from point estimates.

9. **Make serving economics falsifiable.** Freeze a cost model tied to actual billable resources or an explicitly labeled internal resource model. Include retrieval overfetch, projection, inference, ExactScope execution, and rejected-request handling; avoid counting tokens and the GPU time they represent twice. Report resource counts separately from monetary estimates. No production-capacity saving follows automatically from fewer tokens. Freeze one production-like load regime, warm-up, cache treatment, counterbalanced order, and latency measurement scope. Report the 600-query p95 as a measured study gate, not a universal tail-latency guarantee. Compilation and integration are excluded from the serving ratio but included in lifecycle payback.

10. **Freeze failure handling and publication.** Count normal task failures and inappropriate abstentions as failures. Identity corruption or missing observations follows a predetermined invalid-run rule; do not drop inconvenient rows. Never repeat a model call to choose a better answer. Publish ReferenceOnly, invalid, rejected, and inconclusive outcomes along with passes. After any outcome-informed revision, this held-out becomes development evidence for that revision permanently.

**Why not replace FEVER immediately?** The revised selector has never passed its own narrow test, and the current controlled environment can falsify it with limited new integration work. However, a pass based entirely on prompt shortening is evidence for cheaper configuration selection, not Thesis B's semantic layer. Move promptly to real retrieval after a valid pass; do not spend another large cycle expanding FEVER interventions. If credible baseline competence cannot be established, the more useful replacement is the real-retrieval Stage 2 pilot, not a larger weak-baseline benchmark.

## 11. The next three evidence stages

**Stage 1 — within-workload compiler value.**

- **Hypothesis:** the revised frozen selector can produce a strictly cheaper-than-F policy that independently meets the declared quality requirements and, separately, the current product gates.
- **Baseline:** competent unchanged B in the frozen Qwen/FEVER environment.
- **Comparators:** predeclared F and conventional aggregate-constrained tuner T, using the same catalog and calibration observations.
- **Success metric:** all section 10 primary gates pass; report T under identical gates and total selection effort. A comparator tie is compatible with useful tuning but supplies no special-selector advantage.
- **Failure condition:** NoQualifiedReference, ReferenceOnly, inadequate uncertainty bounds, cost/latency failure, or mandatory violation. A valid negative result ends the current claim for this workload; an invalid run establishes nothing and requires root-cause correction before any separately registered replacement.
- **Justified claim if passed:** one fixed same-model policy selected before test reduced serving cost relative to F while meeting specified quality and Base-relative requirements on the declared oracle-pooled frame. No real-retrieval, cross-runtime, or moat claim.

**Stage 2 — customer-like real-retrieval document QA.**

- **Hypothesis:** evidence/contract policies improve actual completed-task economics in a pinned-model application after integration, qualification, and rejection handling are charged.
- **Baseline:** the customer's competent production-like retrieval and generation configuration, with existing caching and output constraints sensibly configured.
- **Comparators:** a predeclared strong fixed intervention recipe; a skilled generic tuning/DSPy-style workflow with a matched offline budget; and a better-model alternative where deployment rules permit it. Offline optimization may use extra models, but serving comparisons obey the customer's call requirements. If a better model cannot be deployed, document the concrete constraint and treat its result as an economic boundary, not a deployable option.
- **Design:** genuine retrieval over authorized versioned documents, including missing evidence, distractors, stale/conflicting sources, unanswerable questions, and long contexts. Freeze answer contracts independently of gold. Use untouched documents/time periods and independently adjudicated answers; no gold-page pool. Keep primary traffic representative and adversarial challenge cases separately labeled. Add a matched prompt-only control or a predeclared removal of the chosen semantic rule to determine whether semantic policy contributes beyond prompt pruning.
- **Success metric:** predeclare customer task-quality and coverage floors, reference-relative loss limits, at least 15% serving savings over the competent fixed reference, no worse total completed-task economics than the incumbent, and the customer's latency requirement. As proposed commercial gates, seek integration within five engineer-days and payback within 90 days at observed eligible volume; validate those limits with the buyer before scoring. Require a temporal prospective run, not just replay, before claiming sustained production value.
- **Failure condition:** apparent savings depend on rejecting useful work, oracle evidence, a weak baseline, uncharged fallback, inaccessible annotations, bespoke repairs, or lifecycle costs that erase payback. If an equally integrated fixed recipe or ordinary tuner provides the same outcome, the product needs a measured workflow advantage to justify itself.
- **Justified claim if passed:** scoped net value for one customer-like service. Repeat with a second independent customer/integrator before claiming repeatable commercial fit. The same stage can be repeated; it does not justify a broad adapter expansion.

**Stage 3 — prospective transfer of accumulated knowledge.**

- **Hypothesis:** source-derived policy/intervention knowledge reduces target search effort while preserving independent qualification yield and serving quality/cost.
- **Baseline:** cold search on a new target with the same catalog, total budget, reference quality floor, and final qualification rules.
- **Comparators:** prior-informed search, the strongest static transferable recipe, and descriptor-blind pooled priors. Include one frozen-workload runtime change and a separate new-workload change. Keep the model and evidence pipeline fixed for the runtime comparison where feasible; otherwise declare a compound change and do not attribute effects to runtime alone.
- **Success metric:** as a proposed preregistered target, at least 30% fewer candidate executions or calibration labels to obtain policies that pass the same fresh final gates, with no material reduction in qualification yield under a declared margin and adequate uncertainty analysis. Report ranking quality, time to qualification, integration hours, failures, and target-level variation. Savings must exceed the added maintenance cost of the prior system.
- **Failure condition:** transfer benefits appear only after target tuning, related examples leak across tasks, all targets require cold exhaustive search, poor targets are omitted, or the apparent benefit vanishes against a simple shared recipe. A runtime header compile or separate per-runtime success is not a pass.
- **Justified claim if passed:** prospective transfer value over the tested targets. Only the broader replication described in section 9 supports an emerging knowledge advantage; it still does not establish a durable moat by itself.

## 12. Commercial wedge

**The strongest initial wedge remains high-volume, bounded enterprise document QA with a genuinely pinned model, authoritative evidence, and measurable recurring costs. Narrow it further to short factual answers, extraction, or typed decisions with citations.** Open-ended synthesis greatly weakens deterministic acceptance and makes quality qualification expensive.

The likely buyer is the team jointly responsible for application quality and inference spend. The relevant trigger is a recurring cost problem in a mature service whose model cannot cheaply be replaced. The qualification artifact is the deliverable; savings, maintained coverage, and low maintenance are the purchase reasons. This buyer hypothesis is not yet supported by demand evidence.

- **Versus on-device/edge retrofit:** edge constraints can make small deterministic processing valuable, but device matrices, native integration, battery/thermal behavior, and release cycles raise validation cost. Reported fixture compatibility is far from a product. Defer unless a concrete buyer supplies a bounded target and workload.
- **Versus generic RAG optimization:** a broad offering collides directly with frameworks, consultants, and evaluation vendors. Restrict ownership to an existing stack's evidence-to-model contract. Do not sell a new retrieval platform.
- **Versus runtime integration tooling:** adapters alone have weak differentiation and a high maintenance burden. Runtime neutrality is useful only when a buyer needs it; it should not be the first purchase reason.
- **Versus general inference cost optimization:** a large budget category but heavily exposed to runtime improvements, model substitution, batching, caching, and pricing changes. ExactScope should compete only where semantic work elimination or evidence shaping adds measurable value after these options are sensibly applied.

Use explicit payback accounting. Let A be incremental integration, calibration, qualification, and initial setup cost; let delta be savings per eligible request after fallback and added host work. When delta is positive, initial break-even volume is `A / delta`. Over a deployment period, require `eligible_volume * delta` to exceed A plus profile refresh, maintenance, and product fees. For self-hosted inference, identify how saved compute produces avoidable capacity expenditure or useful throughput. Otherwise report efficiency, not cash savings.

A pinned model is a constraint to validate, not a permanent moat. If a better-model or simpler runtime change wins on total economics and is deployable, recommend it. ExactScope should not depend on preventing a rational model upgrade.

## 13. What to remove

These are recommendations for subsequent decisions; this review makes no implementation changes.

- **Delete from active claims:** successful Harness Distillation, universal same-model improvement, established runtime neutrality, automatic semantic understanding, and profile-size-as-moat language. Preserve historical negative records.
- **Freeze:** expansion of the intervention rack, new runtime adapters, a public universal ABI, and native footprint optimization until the next evidence gate and a customer constraint justify them.
- **Quarantine:** global C reranking, unqualified lazy fetch/snippet substitution, universal compact prompt defaults, and globally enabled speculation/cache hints. The current research status reports regressions or model-dependent economics; they are development evidence, not defaults.
- **Delegate entirely to the host:** retrieval execution, authorization, corpus/index management, tokenizer, template rendering, exact token accounting, inference, scheduling, batching, KV/prefix cache, constrained decoding, speculative decoding, accelerator policy, retry/fallback, deployment activation, and rollback.
- **Defer:** learned per-request routing, larger search spaces, automatic policy synthesis, cross-workload meta-learning infrastructure, broad zero-call QA, distributed qualification services, dashboards, proprietary tracing, and a hosted control plane.
- **Remove as a separate research objective:** proving that every borrowed runtime feature can be wrapped. A feature enters the catalog only when a specific workload hypothesis and measured economic effect require it.
- **Stop after a valid Stage 1 failure:** further same-cohort selector patching presented as confirmation. Any materially different objective needs fresh evidence and an explicit reason to invest, not continuity with sunk implementation effort.

Aggressive narrowing should remove ownership and unsupported claims while retaining the negative results that prevent repeated mistakes.

## 14. What must remain

The irreducible system is small:

1. A versioned workload/evidence/answer contract with explicit limits on what can be verified.
2. One bounded, host-independent evidence transformation and answer-contract interface that earns its place in a real workload.
3. An immutable candidate policy, exact dependency identity, and independent qualification attestation.
4. A deterministic provisional selector with NoQualifiedReference, ReferenceOnly, and rejection as legitimate outcomes.
5. Frozen paired evaluation against competent Base and Reference, with full serving and lifecycle cost accounting.
6. Strict preparation/finalization failure behavior and request-bound evidence/proof receipts.
7. A small registry of intervention definitions and positive, negative, and inconclusive prospective outcomes.

Proof-based completion is optional until a sound high-volume use case exists. Cross-runtime priors are optional until there are enough independent outcomes to test them. Neither should delay demonstrating value in one existing host.

The registry should begin as an auditable experiment record, not a prematurely built knowledge platform. The semantic executor can remain replaceable. The central strategic asset, if one develops, is the ability to make useful qualified decisions with less customer effort.

## 16. Final recommendation

**Rank 1 — Option 1: Thesis B internally + Thesis C externally + Thesis A as the first conservative compiler algorithm, with strict scope limits.** This preserves a useful semantic boundary while giving the customer a concrete outcome and the next study a falsifiable objective. B remains an internal architecture hypothesis; do not market a newly established category. C describes the intended product, not a claim that qualified savings already exist. A remains replaceable if fresh evidence later favors another declared objective.

**Rank 2 — Option 3: Thesis C without claiming a new technical category.** This becomes preferable if customers value qualification and integration but generic tuning supplies the same policies. A profitable narrow product need not invent a compiler category. It must still prove recurring value beyond bespoke services.

**Rank 3 — Option 4: a substantially modified alternative.** If selection has no advantage but deterministic evidence/answer contracts prove useful, retain a small contract-and-qualification component that integrates into existing optimizers and RAG systems. Price and invest in it according to observed adoption, not the original amplifier ambition.

**Rank 4 — Option 2: Thesis A as the full product.** Too narrow and easy to copy. It risks selling an experimental constraint rather than a customer outcome, with ReferenceOnly as a frequent and commercially disappointing result.

**Rank 5 — Option 5: reject the current direction.** Immediate rejection is premature because a bounded prospective test can resolve a meaningful uncertainty. It becomes the right decision if a competent customer-like study shows that savings disappear after lifecycle costs, ordinary tuning reproduces the result with no additional effort, and semantic policy contributes no independent value. Repeated cold exhaustive search specifically rejects the moat hypothesis even if a small services/product offering remains viable.

Authorize only the next bounded evidence stage before broad implementation resumes. A valid failed Stage 1 should end the current FEVER compiler claim; proceeding to a different workload then requires an independently credible customer opportunity. A passed Stage 1 should move directly to real retrieval rather than trigger a larger feature program.

**Recommended category sentence:**
ExactScope is a workload-specific policy compiler that binds bounded evidence and answer rules to explicit qualification for an existing AI stack.

**Recommended customer sentence:**
ExactScope tests cheaper configurations of your existing document-answering service and delivers a deployable profile only when it meets predeclared quality and cost requirements on fresh evaluation data.

**12-month moat hypothesis:**
Prospectively recorded workload, capability, and intervention outcomes will reduce search or calibration effort by at least 30% on untouched customer workload/host combinations while preserving the same independent qualification standard.

**Single highest-information next experiment:**
Run the modified, fully preregistered Qwen/FEVER study with 120 fresh grouped calibration claims and 600 untouched frame-sampled held-out claims, comparing competent B, predeclared F, paired-preserving P, and conventional tuner T under frozen quality/cost gates, with no reselection, sample extension, or deployment after failed qualification.
